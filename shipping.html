Shipping Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Talos LLC ships to addresses within the United States. This policy outlines our shipping methods, timeframes, and costs.

Free Shipping

Orders with a total of $350 or more qualify for free standard shipping within the contiguous United States. The 15% promotional discount is applied before the shipping threshold is calculated.

Shipping Rates

Orders under $350 are charged a flat shipping rate based on weight and destination. Rates are calculated at checkout before payment.

Processing Time

Orders are typically processed within 1–2 business days. During peak periods, processing may take up to 3 business days.

Delivery Timeframes

  • Standard Shipping: 5–7 business days
  • Expedited Shipping: 2–3 business days (additional fee applies)

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with tracking information. You can also track your order on our Track Order page.

Shipping Restrictions

We currently ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship to PO boxes for oversized or heavy industrial items. Additional restrictions may apply to certain products.

Contact

Shipping questions? Contact Talos LLC, Wyoming, USA via our Contact page.
